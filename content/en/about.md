My mother named me Douglas da Silva Ferreira in Petrópolis, in 1991.

Sometime between the fall of the Soviet Union and the present day, I played more video games than I probably should have, and although I did not grow up surrounded by books at home, I ended up reading a considerable number of them. As a boy, I wanted to be a writer, but so far I have not managed to become one.

Along the way, I collected a few degrees, not that any of that matters much. But if you are the sort of person who cares about these things, I have papers in a folder saying that I hold a bachelor’s degree in Social Sciences from UFRJ, a master’s degree in the History of Science and Technology from the same institution, a postgraduate specialization in Data Science from USP/ESALQ, and that I am currently enrolled in a PhD program in Social Communication at PUC-Rio. What I am most proud of, though, is having been something of a wandering academic, never staying anywhere for too long.

I spent many years teaching at public high schools and also worked as a school principal. Today, I work at the [Instituto Democracia em Xeque](https://institutodx.org/) and at the [Observatory of Algorithms and Information Systems with Social Impact — OASIS/UFRJ](https://oasisufrj.org/), and I am also part of [CondadoLab](https://condadolab.org/).

Besides that, I have been in psychoanalysis for 12 years, and I remain engaged in the seemingly endless pursuit of psychoanalytic training.
